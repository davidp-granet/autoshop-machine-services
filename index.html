We provide machining services on engine components of all sizes, from complete cylinder head rebuilding and crack detection to block boring, cylinder head porting, valve jobs, and welding repair.

What we do:

Cylinder Head Rebuilding

Complete rebuilding for worn, damaged, or performance-focused cylinder heads.

Crack Detection

Magnaflux crack detection on small and large components to help find hidden failures.

Resurfacing

Cylinder head resurfacing for a clean, accurate sealing surface.

Boring & Honing

Engine block boring and honing for proper cylinder dimensions and finish.

Pressure Testing

Pressure testing to help identify leaks and integrity problems before assembly.

Valve Jobs

Complete valve jobs along with seat and guide replacement as needed.

TIG & MIG Welding

Professional welding for stainless, aluminum, titanium, and mild steel components.

Engine Component Machining

General automotive machining on engine components, from common repair work to specialized jobs.

Cylinder Head Porting

Precision cylinder head porting to improve airflow, increase engine efficiency, and support higher-performance builds.

Welding Repair

Professional welding repair for damaged, cracked, or worn metal components, restoring strength and function with durable, precision welds.

Ultrasonic Block Cleaning

Deep cleaning of engine blocks using ultrasonic technology to remove oil, carbon, sludge, and other contaminants from hard-to-reach areas.

Seat and Guide Replacement

Replacement and machining of valve seats and guides to restore proper valve sealing, alignment, and engine performance.
